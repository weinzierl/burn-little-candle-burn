## Numbers in general

- Numbers grouped with spaces are one number: "2 564" is "two thousand five hundred sixty-four", "1 056 540" is "one million fifty-six thousand five hundred forty".
- Decimals digit by digit after the point: "0.0075" is "zero point zero zero seven five", "4.3" is "four point three", "81.7 percent" is "eighty-one point seven percent".
- Versions are not decimals: "0.22" is "zero point twenty-two", not "zero point two two" (see the table).
- Years as years: 2015 "twenty fifteen", 1088 "ten eighty-eight".
- KiB is said "kilobytes" (the slides keep KiB). The script already writes megabytes, milliseconds, megahertz and kilohertz as words.

## Left to the voice, check on the first listen

- "Llama" (LAH-ma), "Candle", "Burn", "Flex", "Metal", "Vulkan", "Adam", "Learner": plain English.
- Single letters as variables: "x", "w", "B". If the voice reads "x" as "times", respell it "ex".
- "Result" and "Results" are the Rust type; ordinary English pronunciation is right.
